About Us

A student-run journal club dedicated to reading, discussing, and presenting cutting-edge research together.

Our Mission

We aim to foster a culture of academic inquiry by providing students with opportunities to engage with primary research literature, develop critical analysis skills, and build a collaborative scholarly community.

Our Vision

We envision a campus where every student has access to the tools and community needed to participate in the global research conversation — regardless of their field or experience level.

What We Do

The core activities that drive our club forward.

Researching Help

Our club leaders are extremely experienced in research. The club teaches students how to research effectively and guides them completely throughout the process. Research is a difficult skill but with guidance, anyone can become a pro.

Competition Readiness

Throughout the school year, the club participates in many different researching competitions. The club aims to help members become prepared enough and guides them throughout the process ensuring that they create a powerful final product.
