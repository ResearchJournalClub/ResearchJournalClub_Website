About the Research Journal Club

We are a community of students passionate about scientific inquiry. Our journal club meets regularly to read peer-reviewed papers, discuss methodologies, and present findings.

Our Activities

A glimpse into what our club actually does.

Monthly Research Topics

The club will choose a topic to research for the month. Members will work with their peers to create a high quality product.

Peer Based Work

We believe that the best way to learn is through collaboration with your peers. All members will have a chance to work with their peers and learn from everybody in the club.

Competitions

Throughout the year, the club will participate in various different research competitions. These competitions will feature various backgrounds such as biology, computer science, and ethnic studies. Competitions are a great way to evaluate yourself against your peers and are great ways to improve your research skills.
