I.

Example Project

This poster explores the application of Marker-Assisted Selection (MAS) in livestock breeding to manage zoonotic diseases and enhance resistance to disease. By identifying specific genetic markers (QTLs) linked to desirable phenotypic traits, breeders can select for animals with inherent disease resistance, early in life, rather than the slow process of traditional breeding. The work follows the history of MAS from its theoretical basis in 1923 to genomic selection and fine-mapping approaches. It points to commercial applications (such as Zoetis's INHERIT Select and Topigs Norsvin's PRRS-resistant swine breeding) but also to major challenges, including high genotyping costs, regulatory scrutiny, patent access, and ethical concerns about artificial selection.

Research Ideas

These are some example prompts/topics that are similar to what we research.

1

How Is AI Changing Education?

Possible Prompts:

  • Does AI improve or hurt student learning?
  • How should schools approach AI-assisted work?
  • Can AI-generated information be reliably fact-checked?
2

How Does Sleep Affect Student Performance?

Possible Prompts:

  • What does existing research say about sleep and memory?
  • How does sleep duration relate to concentration?
  • Why do teenagers have different sleep patterns?
3

How Are Cities Preparing for Extreme Heat?

Possible Prompts:

  • How do trees and shade affect urban temperatures?
  • Which strategies reduce heat exposure?
  • Do all neighborhoods experience heat equally?
